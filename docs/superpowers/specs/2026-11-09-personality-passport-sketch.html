Make-your-own passport: sketch v3

The questions are now inside the passport, one page each. Click through it in the phone.

  1. Open it. Tap the closed passport. It opens like a book, then turns into the stacked layout.
  2. One page per question. The bottom page has the question and its 4 answers in rows. The top page shows that question's image (random placeholder photos here).
  3. Flip. Tap an answer; it turns green and the page flips up to the next question. Tap the top page to flip back one.
  4. Name page. The last page asks for their name, filled in from registration.
  5. Reveal. The final flip shows their page filling itself in: character, type, ticks, "likely to say". The stamp page is on top.
  6. Stamps. Press "Pretend a booth scanned me".
Still placeholders: the questions, the 4 types, the scene pictures (emoji here) and the character drawing. The organiser would supply real art per event. Page flips are stiff here; the real build would curl the paper.
